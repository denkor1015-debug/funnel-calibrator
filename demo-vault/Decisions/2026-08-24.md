Measured approval is 59.01% and buyout 45.26% against the assumed 65% / 52.5%,
across 95 resolved orders (161 leads, 20 excluded as immature cohort) with
reliability `high`. That drops contribution to 270.49 ₴ from an assumed 306.14 ₴,
a drift of −30.6%, and pulls the true Stop CPL down to $1.61 with a Goal of
$1.12 — so `target_above_breakeven` is **true**: the $1.63 goal is above the
$1.61 break-even. At the current $1.63 the margin is −$0.02 per lead and the
audit reports the cost sits 45.5% over the true goal; with approval and buyout
both near assumption and no competitor signal, the diagnosis is
`weak_offer_or_creative` and the counter-recommendation is `stop`. This is
directly the open question from yesterday's note — nobody had looked at what
happens to these orders after the call.

`new_creative`, because only about a third of these cheap leads confirm on the
call. Treat this as directional only — on 31 resolved orders the evidence is thin
enough that the rate should be re-measured before any spend is moved on the
strength of it.

This is the strongest sample of the three: 206 resolved orders from 311 leads

current $6.00 is still 75.4% over the true goal and loses $1.11 per lead, so the

counter-recommendation is `pause_retry`, not `stop`. Stopping would retire a
product whose funnel is performing better than the portfolio assumes.
